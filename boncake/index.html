How to order

Good to know

Can I just buy a slice?

Yes. Slices and drinks are at the counter every day we're open. Seating is shared with the food court.

How much notice for a whole cake?

At least 2 days is safest. Give us more time before weekends and holidays.

Can you write a message on the cake?

Yes. Tell us when you order and we'll confirm it with you.

Where do I park?

First Markham Place's lot gets busy at lunch and on weekends. Give yourself a few extra minutes.
