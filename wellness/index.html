How we work

Three things we won’t trade away.

Stillwater is deliberately small. Four rooms, four practitioners, and a front desk that knows who you are when you call.

靜水流深

“Still water runs deep” — the four characters over our door, and the way we like to work.

  1. i.

    Unhurried appointments

    A 60-minute session is 60 minutes of hands-on time. We leave fifteen minutes between visits, so nobody is watching the door — not your practitioner, and not you.

  2. ii.

    One practitioner, start to finish

    The person who greets you is the person who treats you. No hand-off to an assistant halfway through, and the same practitioner next time unless you ask for a change.

  3. iii.

    Speak to us in your language

    Booking, explanations and receipts in English, Cantonese or Mandarin. Bring a parent who would rather not have their appointment translated for them.

Your first visit

No surprises. Here is what happens.

  1. 110 minutes before

    Arrive and settle

    Park at the door and have a cup of warm tea. There is one short form — on paper, on a clipboard, handed straight to your practitioner.

  2. 2In the room

    Talk first

    With the door closed, your practitioner asks about your health and what you would like from the session — in the language you prefer.

  3. 3The session

    Explained as it goes

    You hear what is about to happen before it does. Say “softer”, “firmer” or “stop” at any point and that is what happens.

  4. 4Before you go

    Billing, then home

    We bill your plan directly where we can, or hand you a receipt to claim. Rebook if you want to; nobody will press you.

Wear something comfortable. If you would like us to bill your plan, bring your benefits card — that is all.

The practical part

Insurance, receipts and changing your plans.

Direct billing

We bill most extended health plans directly, so you pay only the part your plan does not cover. We will tell you when we confirm your booking whether yours is one of them.

Receipts for claims

Every visit comes with an itemised receipt: practitioner, credentials and registration number, date, session length and fee — what insurers ask for.

24-hour cancellation

Plans change. Give us 24 hours’ notice and there is no charge. Under 24 hours, half the session fee applies. If you wake up unwell, call us; we would rather you rest.
