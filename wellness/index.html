Direct billing

  • Sun Life
  • Manulife
  • Canada Life
  • Green Shield

We bill most group insurance plans directly, including Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life and Green Shield. You pay only the part your plan does not cover.

Bring your insurance card to your first visit.

If we can’t bill your plan, you get a receipt with your practitioner’s registration number so you can claim it yourself.

Car accident or workplace injury (WSIB)? Call us before your first visit so we can set up the paperwork.

Some plans need a doctor’s note before they pay for physiotherapy or massage. Check with your plan first.

Your first visit

  1. Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short form at the front desk.
  2. Bring photo ID and your insurance card.
  3. Wear loose, comfortable clothes. Shorts help for physiotherapy.
  4. Your practitioner asks about your health in person, in a private room, before treatment starts.
