Insurance and direct billing

We can bill most group insurance plans directly, so you only pay the part your plan does not cover. Bring your insurance card to your first visit.

Every visit comes with a receipt showing your practitioner’s registration number, so you can claim from plans we cannot bill.

Car accident and workplace injury claims: call us before your first visit so we can set up the paperwork.

Some plans need a doctor’s note before they pay for physiotherapy or massage. Check with your plan first.

Your first visit

  • Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short form.
  • Bring photo ID and your insurance card.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothes. Shorts are handy for physiotherapy.
  • Your practitioner asks about your health in person, in a private room.

Good to know

Parking
Free parking in our lot, with two accessible spots by the door.
Accessibility
We are on the ground floor with no steps. Wide doors, an accessible washroom and treatment tables that lower.
Cancelling
Please give us 24 hours’ notice to cancel or move an appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments are charged half the fee.

Debit, Visa, Mastercard, cash or Interac e-Transfer. Massage, physiotherapy and acupuncture are HST-exempt. HST is added to osteopathic manual therapy.
