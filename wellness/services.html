Questions people ask us

Do I need a doctor’s referral?
Not for us. Some insurance plans need one before they pay, so check your plan.
How soon can I get in?
Usually within a week. Evening times fill up first.
Do you have evening appointments?
Yes, until 8 pm Monday to Friday. Saturdays we close at 4 pm.
Can I bring someone with me?
Yes. A family member or friend can wait in the waiting area or come into the room with you.
Do you treat children?
Physiotherapy and massage, yes. Patients under 16 need a parent or guardian with them.

Direct billing

We bill most group insurance plans directly, including Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life and Green Shield. You pay only the part your plan does not cover.

If we can’t bill your plan, you get a receipt with your practitioner’s registration number so you can claim it yourself.

Some plans need a doctor’s note before they pay for physiotherapy or massage. Check with your plan first.

Ask for a time that suits you

Tell us the service and two times that work for you. We call back within one business day to confirm.
