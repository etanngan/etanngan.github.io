Questions people ask us

Do I need a doctor’s referral?
Not for us. Some insurance plans need one before they pay, so check your plan.
How soon can I get in?
Usually within a week. Evening times fill up first.
Can I bring someone with me?
Yes. A family member or friend can wait in the waiting area or join you in the room.
Do you treat children?
Physiotherapy and massage, yes. Clients under 16 need a parent or guardian with them.

Insurance and direct billing

We can bill most group insurance plans directly, so you only pay the part your plan does not cover. Bring your insurance card to your first visit.
