Good to know before you come

Do you take cards?

No — T&T is cash only. Please bring cash with you.

How long is the wait?

On busy weekend mornings, hot food can take 20–30 minutes. Bakery items are ready right away, and you can call ahead for whole pies.

Are you related to T&T Supermarket?

No. We're an independent, family-run bakery café on Main Street — no connection to the supermarket chain.

Can I order a whole pie or cake?

Yes. Call the café, or send a holiday pie and tart request online.
