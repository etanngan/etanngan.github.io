Good to know

Do I need to order ahead?

For the Peking duck combo, yes please. We make it fresh and it gets busy, so calling ahead saves you a wait.

Where do I park?

Spots outside fill up fast. Many people park at First Markham Place and walk over. Take care crossing Fairburn Drive.

Are you inside First Markham Place?

Not quite: we're in the plaza at 11 Fairburn Drive, Unit 19, just south of First Markham Place.

What sauce comes with the duck?

Roast duck comes with plum sauce. The Peking duck combo comes with its own sauces for the wrappers.
