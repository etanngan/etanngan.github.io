What makes us different

Five things we do that you can check.

  1. Six seats per class. Really.

    Every group class is capped at six. When the sixth seat goes, the timetable shows “waitlist” — we open a second section rather than squeeze in a seventh chair.

  2. Lead teachers are Ontario-certified.

    Each class is planned and taught by an Ontario-certified teacher who has taught that grade in a school classroom. Assistants help in the homework club; they don’t lead lessons.

  3. A progress note every month, in your language.

    One page, first week of the month: topics covered, a quick-check score, what’s secure, what needs work, and one thing to try at home. English, Traditional or Simplified Chinese — you choose.

  4. We follow the Ontario classroom — not ahead of it for show.

    We ask for your child’s course outline and follow the curriculum strand by strand, including de-streamed Grade 9 math (MTH1W). Enrichment comes once the school material is secure, not instead of it.

  5. A homework club with a real sign-out sheet.

    Doors open at 3:15. Homework first, then reading. Pick up any time from 5:30 to 6:30 pm; children are signed out only to an adult on your list.

What we won’t promise: a guaranteed mark. What we will: you’ll always know exactly where your child stands.

How it works

From first call to first progress note.

  1. Free placement assessment

    About 40 minutes, by appointment. Your child works through grade-level questions while a teacher watches how they think — not just the score.

  2. Small-group placement

    We suggest a class by level and timetable — and tell you plainly if we don’t have the right group this term.

  3. Weekly classes

    Same teacher, same time, every week. Try one class first if you like: $30, credited to your first month.

  4. Monthly progress note

    First week of each month, in the language you prefer. Questions? Book a 10-minute call with the teacher.
