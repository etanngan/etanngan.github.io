How to start

  1. Call us or send an inquiry

    Tell us your child’s grade and what they need help with.

  2. Free assessment, about 40 minutes

    Your child does a short test with a teacher. Bring their latest report card if you can.

  3. Try one class for $30

    If it is a good fit, register for the rest of the term. We take the $30 off your first payment.

For parents

Drop-off and pick-up
Use the 10-minute spots by the main door, not the fire lane. An adult signs out students in Grades 1 to 3 at the front desk.
School pick-up for Homework Club
Our staff walk students over from three nearby elementary schools. Call us to check if your child’s school is one of them.
Missed classes
Tell us by noon on the day of the class. Your child can join another section of the same course that week. Missed classes are not refunded.
Sick days
If your child is sick but well enough to learn, they can join their class by video call. Ask the front desk for the link.
Payment
Interac e-Transfer, debit, Visa, Mastercard or cheque. Term fees are due before the first class. Homework Club is paid monthly.
Snacks and allergies
We are a nut-free centre. Homework Club students can bring a snack. Please tell us about any allergies when you register.
Progress updates
You get a short written note every month and a 15-minute meeting with the teacher each term.
