    For parents

    Pickup, parking, missed classes and the other things parents ask us about most.

    Drop-off and pickup
    Pull into the six 10-minute spots by the main door, not the fire lane. Grades 1 to 3 wait inside until an adult signs them out at the front desk. Grade 4 and up can walk out to your car once you have told us in writing.
    School pickup for Homework Club
    Our staff walk students over from three nearby elementary schools at 3:20 pm. Call us to check if your child’s school is one of them.
    Missed classes
    Tell us by noon on the day. Your child can join another section of the same course that week. Missed classes are not refunded.
    Sick days
    If your child is sick but well enough to learn, they can join their class by video call. Ask the front desk for the link.
    Payment
    Interac e-Transfer, debit, Visa, Mastercard or cheque. Term fees are due before the first class. Homework Club is paid monthly.
    Snacks and allergies
    We are a nut-free centre. Homework Club students can bring a snack. Please tell us about allergies when you register.
    Progress updates
    The marked work comes home every week. You also get a short written note every month and a 15-minute meeting with the teacher each term.
