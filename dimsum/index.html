Before you visit

Weekend waits
Weekends from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm are our busiest. Expect to wait 30 to 45 minutes. Take a number at the front desk and we will text you when your table is ready.
Tea charge
$2.00 per person, or $1.50 for seniors 65 and over. Leave the teapot lid open and we will bring more hot water.
Weekday morning prices
Monday to Friday before 11:00 am, all small, medium and large dim sum are $5.50. Not on holidays.
Payment
Cash, debit, Visa, Mastercard, WeChat Pay and Alipay. We do not take American Express. On weekends we print one bill per table.
Large groups
Groups of 10 or more can book any day. A 15% service charge is added for groups of 10 or more.
Wheelchairs and children
Step-free entrance and an accessible washroom. We have high chairs and booster seats.
Allergies
Our kitchen uses shrimp, shellfish, peanuts, sesame, wheat, soy and egg, and the steamers are shared. Please tell your server about any allergy before you order.
