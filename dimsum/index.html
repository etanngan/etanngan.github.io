Booking a table
Call us at (905) 555-0138. On weekdays we book any size of table. On weekends we only book tables of 6 or more; smaller tables take a number at the front desk.
Tea charge
$2.00 per person, $1.50 for seniors 65 and over. Leave the teapot lid open and we bring more hot water.
Paying the bill
Cash, debit, Visa, Mastercard, WeChat Pay and Alipay. No American Express. Pay at the front counter; on weekends it is one bill per table.
Parking
Free parking in the plaza. On weekends the front lot is full by noon, so use the lot behind the building.
Groups of 10 or more
Book any day, weekends too. A 15% service charge is added for 10 or more.
Takeout
Call ahead to order. Most orders are ready in about 20 minutes. Pick up and pay at the front counter. (905) 555-0138
Wheelchairs and children
Step-free entrance and an accessible washroom. High chairs and booster seats are available.
Allergies
Our kitchen uses shrimp, shellfish, peanuts, sesame, wheat, soy and egg, and the steamers are shared. Tell your server about any allergy before you order.

No holiday changes coming up.

Private room for family parties

Seats up to 30 at three round tables. We host birthdays, baby full-month parties and wedding tea ceremonies.

Set dinners start at $388 for a table of 10. A $100 deposit holds the date.
