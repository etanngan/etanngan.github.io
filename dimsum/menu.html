    Allergies

    Our kitchen handles shrimp, shellfish, peanuts, sesame, wheat, soy and egg on the same steamers and woks, so we cannot promise any dish is free of traces. Vegetarian dishes may be cooked alongside meat. Please tell your server about allergies before you order.
