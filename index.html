Markham Trilingual Web

Websites for Markham businesses in English and Chinese

Here are three example sites for made-up local businesses: a dim sum restaurant, a tutoring centre and a clinic. Every page works in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

Try the language buttons at the top. Your choice carries over into all three sites.

Jade Basket Dim Sum House

翠籠軒, a dim sum restaurant

A restaurant site that answers what people check before they come: today’s hours, parking, weekend waits and the tea charge.

What it has

  • A full menu priced by dim sum size, in all three languages
  • A QR code for each table that opens a phone menu with search and a list to show the server
  • Holiday hours through Lunar New Year, payment methods and a private room for parties

Maple Bridge Learning Centre

楓橋教育中心, an after-school tutoring centre

A tutoring site that shows parents the fees, the teachers and every class with seats left, before they pick up the phone.

What it has

  • A weekly timetable parents can filter by grade, subject and day
  • An inquiry form that carries over the class they picked and doesn’t ask for the child’s name or school
  • Pick-up and parking rules, missed-class policy, term dates and PA day camps

Stillwater Wellness Clinic

靜水堂, a physiotherapy, massage and acupuncture clinic

A clinic site that puts prices, insurance and parking up front, and takes appointment requests without asking about anyone’s health online.

What it has

  • Every service with session lengths, prices and who you will see
  • A booking request form with a calendar that skips Sundays and holidays, and no health questions
  • Direct billing, first-visit checklist, cancellation policy and holiday hours

What every site includes

  • Three languagesEnglish, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, written separately so each reads naturally. The site remembers the visitor’s choice.
  • The basics firstHours, address, parking and phone number at the top of the home page, with today’s hours highlighted.
  • Made for phonesTap to call, large buttons and forms that work on small screens.
  • Map and directionsA map in the visitor’s language and plain directions by car and by bus.
  • Holiday hoursA table of closures and short days, so customers don’t show up to a locked door.
  • Forms that helpClear error messages in every language, and only the questions a business actually needs.
